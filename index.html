About RAWE

Rural Agricultural Work Experience Programme

The Rural Agricultural Work Experience (RAWE) Programme provides students with practical exposure to rural life, farming systems and agricultural practices.


The programme helps students interact with farmers, understand village-level agricultural problems, study farming practices and gain practical experience in agricultural development.


The Paiyur Team participates in field activities, farmer interactions, surveys, demonstrations and other agricultural extension activities.
