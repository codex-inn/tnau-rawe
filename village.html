TNAU research and farmer-support centre

🌱 RRS Paiyur – Summary

Tamil Nadu Agricultural University established the Regional Research Station at Paiyur on 23 May 1973, in the erstwhile Dharmapuri district, now Krishnagiri district.

The station focuses on agricultural research, technology generation and practical support for the farming systems of the region.

🔬 Research Focus

Crop Research: Finger millet, horse gram, mochai and other regional crops.
Farming Systems: Technology generation for rainfed and irrigated agriculture.
Soil & Water: Soil management and moisture conservation.
Horticulture: Tropical fruit, mango, vegetable and flower crop management.
Crop Protection: Pest and disease surveillance with IPM/IDM components.
Technology Testing: Verification work for rice, sorghum, groundnut and other crops.
