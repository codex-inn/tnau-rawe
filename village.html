Paiyur Village Profile

A concise overview of the Paiyur agricultural environment and RRS

📍 Paiyur Village – Summary

Paiyur is an important agricultural locality associated with the Regional Research Station of Tamil Nadu Agricultural University. The area provides a useful field environment for understanding local agriculture, crop production and farmer-oriented agricultural activities.

The Paiyur area is particularly relevant for RAWE students because it connects classroom learning with field observation, farmer interaction, crop studies and practical agricultural experience.

📍 Location Paiyur, Krishnagiri District, Tamil Nadu
🌾 Agricultural Setting Rainfed and irrigated farming environment
🌱 RAWE Relevance Field observation, surveys and farmer interaction
🏫 Major Institution Regional Research Station, Paiyur

🔬 Regional Research Station (RRS), Paiyur

The Regional Research Station, Paiyur was established by Tamil Nadu Agricultural University on 23 May 1973. It was established to undertake agricultural research relevant to the region and to support improved agricultural practices and farm productivity.

📅 Established 23 May 1973
📍 Location Paiyur, Krishnagiri District, Tamil Nadu
📐 Total Area 18.51 ha
🌱 Cultivable Area 17.51 ha
💧 Irrigated Area 6.07 ha
🌾 Rainfed Area 11.44 ha
🌍 Elevation 490 m MSL
🌐 Coordinates 12.21° N, 78.18° E
🌧️ Average Annual Rainfall 917 mm
🌡️ Mean Temperature 21.9°C – 33.9°C
🛣️ Accessibility NH-44 corridor
🏢 Institutional Role Research, technology generation and farmer support

🌱 RRS Paiyur – Research Focus

🌾 Crop Research: Finger millet, horse gram, mochai and other regional crops.
🌱 Farming Systems: Rainfed and irrigated agricultural technologies.
💧 Soil & Water: Moisture conservation, soil management and STCR-related work.
🥭 Horticulture: Mango, tropical fruit, vegetables and flower crops.
🐛 Crop Protection: Pest and disease management and field surveillance.
🧪 Field Research: Crop testing, verification and technology evaluation.
