Regional Research Station – Paiyur

A research and farmer-support centre of Tamil Nadu Agricultural University

🌱 About RRS Paiyur

Tamil Nadu Agricultural University established the Regional Research Station at Paiyur on 23 May 1973 in the erstwhile Dharmapuri district, now Krishnagiri district.

The station was established to undertake intensive research on important crops of the region and to support farmers through appropriate agricultural practices, improved productivity and better farm prosperity.

1973 Established by TNAU

🔬 Research & Development Focus

The official RRS Paiyur objectives include crop research, technology generation, soil and water management, horticulture, vegetable and flower crops, and pest and disease management.

🌾 Lead centre for finger millet, horse gram and mochai
🌱 Technology generation for rainfed and irrigated farming
💧 STCR recommendations and in-situ moisture conservation
🌍 Problem-soil reclamation and polluted-water management
🥭 Tropical fruit crop and mango orchard management
🥬 Vegetable and flower crop management technologies
🐛 Pest and disease surveillance and IPM/IDM components
🌾 Test verification for rice, sorghum, groundnut and other crops
