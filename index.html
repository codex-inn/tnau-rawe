About RAWE

The Rural Agricultural Work Experience (RAWE) Programme provides students with practical exposure to rural life, farming systems and agricultural practices.


Students interact with farmers, understand agricultural practices and gain practical experience through field-level activities.


Learn × Experience × Serve × Grow

Village Profile

🌾 Regional Research Station – Krishnagiri

Established by TNAU in 1973 to conduct crop research and support farmers. It covers 18.51 ha, with red loamy sandy soil.

Average annual rainfall is 917 mm, with temperatures ranging from 21.9°C to 33.9°C.
